Frequently Asked Questions

Are these productivity and schedule tools free to use?

Yes, all PlanBot online tools are 100% free with no sign-up or credit card required. You can generate custom schedules, calculate exam revision allocations, and copy results directly.

How do these online tools compare to the PlanBot Android app?

These web tools generate static schedules and calculations. The PlanBot Android app is an active AI assistant that dynamically adapts your schedule when tasks run late, tracks your streaks/XP, logs analytics, and sends focus notifications.

Yes! You can copy the generated timetable with one click to your clipboard, print or save as a PDF, or download the PlanBot Android app for automated daily tracking.
