PlanBot God Tier Review: Unlimited AI Planning

By Naitik Baldaniya•Published: 2026-06-03•7 min read

Quick Answer: PlanBot God tier (₹249/month or ₹2,499/year) is the highest subscription level, offering unlimited AI plans, unlimited messages, Premium AI (the most advanced model), weekly AI performance reports, OCR photo input for handwritten notes, and a 3-day free trial. It is designed for power users who want maximum AI capability and zero usage limits.

PlanBot offers an incredibly generous Free tier. For many users, 2 AI plans a day is all they need. But for power users—CEOs, advanced competitive exam aspirants, and professional athletes—there is the God Tier.

At ₹249/month (or ₹2499/year), God Tier unlocks the full, unbridled power of PlanBot. But is it actually worth it? Let's break down exactly what you get.

1. The Premium AI Engine

The most significant upgrade in the God Tier is the underlying AI.

While the Free and Pro tiers use excellent, highly optimized models, the God Tier routes your prompts to our Premium AI Engine. This model has vastly superior reasoning capabilities.

If you give a complex prompt with conflicting dependencies—e.g., "I need to prepare for my board meeting, but I also have to pick up my kids at 3 PM, and I need 90 minutes of deep work where I cannot be disturbed"—the Premium AI handles it flawlessly. It understands extreme nuance and context.

2. Unlimited AI Plans

The Free tier limits you to 2 generations per day. God Tier removes the training wheels. You can generate, regenerate, and tweak your daily plans an unlimited number of times.

3. Image Uploads (The Secret Weapon)

This is a God Tier exclusive. Instead of typing out your tasks, you can simply take a photo of your handwritten notes, a syllabus, or a whiteboard.

The Premium AI's multimodal capabilities will read the image, extract the actionable tasks, and automatically build a daily schedule around them. It is arguably the fastest way to plan a day currently available on a mobile device.

4. Advanced Weekly Reports

Elite and God tiers both get Weekly AI Reports. But God Tier reports go deeper. The AI doesn't just show you charts; it provides a comprehensive text analysis of your behavioral patterns, pointing out specific days and times where your productivity dips, and offering psychological strategies to fix it.

The Verdict

If you are a casual user who just wants to organize a few errands, stick to the Free tier. It's fantastic.

But if your time is highly valuable—if you are studying for an exam that dictates your career, or running a business where efficiency equals revenue—the God Tier is a no-brainer. The time saved by the Premium AI and the Image Upload feature pays for the ₹249 subscription in the first 24 hours.

Curious? The God Tier comes with a 3-Day Free Trial. Download PlanBot and try it today.
