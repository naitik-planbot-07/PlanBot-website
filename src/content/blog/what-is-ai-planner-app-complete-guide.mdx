## What is an AI Planner App?

An **AI planner app** is a mobile or web application that uses artificial intelligence to automatically create personalized daily schedules, task lists, and goal-tracking plans for its users. Unlike traditional to-do apps where you manually input every task, an AI planner uses machine learning models to generate optimized plans based on your goals, constraints, and preferences.

**In simple terms:** You tell the AI what you want to achieve. The AI tells you exactly what to do, when to do it, and for how long.

## How Do AI Planner Apps Work?

AI planners use **large language models (LLMs)** — cutting-edge AI technology — to understand natural language goals and generate structured plans.

Here's the process:

1. **Input**: You describe your goal in plain language (*"Help me prepare for NEET in 6 months"*)
2. **Processing**: The AI model analyzes your goal, timeline, available hours, and preferences
3. **Generation**: The model creates a structured daily plan with time blocks, priorities, and breaks
4. **Tracking**: The app tracks your progress, streaks, and completion rates
5. **Adaptation**: Based on your performance data, the AI adjusts future plans
