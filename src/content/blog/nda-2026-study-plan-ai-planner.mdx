## Why NDA Is Different From Other Entrance Exams

NDA (National Defence Academy) is the joint-services entrance exam for the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force — conducted twice a year by UPSC. It's unique among competitive exams for two reasons:

1. **It's a two-stage selection** — the written exam (900 marks) is only half the battle. The 5-day SSB interview is the real test. You must clear both.
2. **It favors 11th/12th students** — the syllabus is Class 11-12 level, so aspirants start young (often Class 11). This means you compete with a serious, prepared cohort.

Over **5-6 lakh candidates** appear for NDA each cycle, for roughly 400 seats. But here's the opportunity: most candidates under-prepare for Math (worth 300 marks) and ignore the SSB entirely until the written result.

If you plan for both stages from day one, you're in the top 10% strategically.

## Understanding the NDA Written Exam Pattern

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mathematics | 120 | 300 | 2.5 hours |
| 2 | General Ability Test (GAT) | 150 | 600 | 2.5 hours |

**Total:** 900 marks · Negative marking (0.33 per wrong answer in both papers) · English-only exam (except some language questions)

### GAT Breakdown (600 marks)

| Section | Marks | Weightage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | 200 | 33% |
| Physics | 100 (part of 400 GK) | ~17% |
| Chemistry | 60 | ~10% |
| General Science (Bio) | 40 | ~7% |
| History, Freedom Movement | 80 | ~13% |
| Geography | 80 | ~13% |
| Current Events | 40 | ~7% |

**Key insight:** Math (300) + English (200) = 500 of 900 marks (56%). Master these two and you're past the cutoff.

## The 6-Month NDA Master Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)

**Objective:** Complete Class 11-12 NCERT for all subjects + Math mastery

**Daily Study Target:** 5-6 hours

**Section Split:**

- Mathematics: 2-2.5 hours/day (non-negotiable priority)
- English: 1 hour/day
- Physics: 1 hour/day
- GK (History, Geo, Chem, Bio): 1 hour/day (rotate)
- Current Affairs: 20-30 min/day

**Mathematics Strategy (the make-or-break paper):**

NDA Math is Class 11-12 level but tests *speed and accuracy*. 120 questions in 150 minutes = 75 seconds per question.

- **High-weightage topics:** Matrices & Determinants, Vectors, Calculus (Differential + Integral), Probability, Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry
- **Lower priority:** Statistics, Set Theory (quick wins)
- Practice 80-100 questions daily once fundamentals are clear
- Build a formula sheet — revise daily
- Source: NCERT Class 11-12 + R.S. Aggarwal for practice

**English Strategy:**

- Vocabulary is king — 50 words daily (NDA tests synonyms, antonyms, idioms)
- Grammar: Parts of Speech, Tenses, Prepositions, Articles
- Comprehension: 2-3 passages daily
- Sentence Improvement, Spotting Errors — daily drill

**Physics Strategy:**

- Class 11-12 NCERT, but NDA tests *conceptual application*
- High-weightage: Mechanics, Optics, Electricity, Modern Physics
- Make a "laws and formulas" sheet

### Phase 3: Mocks + SSB Foundation (Month 6)

**Objective:** Full-length mock series + light SSB prep

**Daily Study Target:** 6 hours

**What Changes:**

- 4-5 full-length NDA mocks per week (Math + GAT back-to-back, simulating real exam)
- Deep analysis after each mock
- Start light SSB preparation (don't neglect this — many written qualifiers fail SSB)
- Current Affairs final revision

**SSB Foundation (light, 30-45 min/day):**

- Read about the 5-day SSB process (Screening, Psych tests, GTO, Interview, Conference)
- Start reading newspapers editorially (builds awareness for interview)
- Light physical fitness (running, basic exercises)

**Why start SSB prep now?** SSB is 5 days of intense psychological + physical testing. You can't prepare for it in the 2-3 weeks between written result and SSB call. Build habits now.

## The Physical Fitness Angle (Unique to NDA)

Unlike other exams, NDA requires you to be physically fit. Start early:

- **Daily:** 20-30 min running/jogging
- **3x/week:** Push-ups, sit-ups, pull-ups (build to 15+ each)
- **Weekend:** Longer runs (3-5 km)

## Common NDA Mistakes That Cost Selection

- **Neglecting Math** — it's 33% of marks and the highest-scoring paper for serious aspirants
- **Ignoring SSB until written result** — SSB has a ~10% selection rate; you need months of prep
