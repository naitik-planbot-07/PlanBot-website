## 📝 Best Note-Taking: Notion

**Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.2/5)**
**Price: Free / Plus $10/mo**
**Platform: Web, Android, iOS**

Notion is the Swiss Army knife of productivity — notes, databases, wikis, project management, and more. Its flexibility is both its strength and weakness; it takes time to set up but rewards power users enormously.

**Best for:** Knowledge management, team collaboration, building custom systems.

**India-specific note:** Notion's free tier is generous and works well for individual students and professionals. The paid plans are in USD, which may not be ideal for budget-conscious users.

## ✅ Best To-Do List: Todoist

**Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.1/5)**
**Price: Free / Pro ₹333/mo**
**Platform: All platforms**

Todoist is the gold standard for task management. Clean interface, natural language input, labels, filters, and projects. It does one thing exceptionally well: keeping track of your tasks.

**Limitation:** Todoist records tasks but doesn't plan your day. You still need to decide what to do first, how long each task takes, and when to do it. That's where AI planners like PlanBot complement Todoist perfectly.

**Best for:** People who want a reliable, cross-platform task recorder.

## 📚 Best Flashcard App: Anki

**Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5)**
**Price: Free (Android)**
**Platform: Android, Desktop (iOS version is paid)**
