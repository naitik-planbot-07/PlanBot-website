## How We Judged (No Sponsored Picks)

Most "best planner app" listicles rank whatever pays the affiliate fee. The test here is simpler and Indian-student-first:

- **Does it plan, or just store?** An app that holds your task list is a notebook. An app that builds your day is a planner.
- **Exam workflow:** does it understand syllabus coverage, revision cycles, and weak subjects - or only generic to-dos?
- **India reality:** INR pricing (USD x 12 months on a student budget hurts), low-end Android performance, and offline behavior.
- **Honest pricing:** verified prices as of September 2026, marked where billing is annual-only.

## 1. PlanBot - Best for Exam-Driven AI Planning

**What it is:** an Android app built around one job - you tell it your goals (JEE 2027, NEET, UPSC, or plain work tasks), and it generates your day every morning and rebalances when life happens.

**Where it wins for students:**

- **Daily AI generation**, not a static timetable - missed chapters carry forward automatically
- **Exam personas and subject weighting** - weak subjects get heavier blocks without you re-planning
- **Spaced revision on completed topics** (higher tiers), the thing students most consistently fail to do manually
- **INR pricing:** free tier, paid tiers from Rs 99 - no USD surprise on your statement
- A suite of **free web tools** (schedule generator, exam revision calculator) that work without the app

**Where it does not win:** it is Android-only right now, and if your world is group projects and shared workspaces rather than exams, a task manager fits better than a day planner.

## 2. Todoist - Best Task List, If You Enjoy Planning Yourself

Todoist is the most polished task manager on any platform, and its natural-language capture ("Physics DPP tomorrow 6pm") is still best-in-class. Pro is **$5/month billed yearly ($60/year, roughly Rs 4,400 a year)** - honest value for a general organizer.

**But note what Todoist does not do:** it will not decide what to study today, rotate your subjects, or reschedule around your energy. You feed it the plan; it stores the plan. For students who already have a system and want a beautiful place to keep it, that is perfect - for students who need the plan built, that is the missing half.

## 3. TickTick - Todoist's Practical Cousin

TickTick bundles a calendar, a pomodoro timer, and a habit tracker around a solid task list, and its free tier is generous. It shares Todoist's fundamental shape though: you do the planning, it does the storing. If you want pomodoro built in and do not need AI generation, it is a strong free start - check their current premium pricing in-app as it changes by region and promotion.

## 4. Notion - Best Build-Your-Own Study HQ

Notion is where engineering students build glorious dashboards with attendance trackers, CGPA calculators, and semester boards. The free personal plan is genuinely generous. The catch is the cost is your time: every template needs maintaining, and "planning" in Notion means you updating databases by hand at 11pm. Use it as your knowledge base; do not confuse it with a daily planner.

## 5. Motion - The AI Scheduler (Priced for Companies)

Motion is the closest thing to PlanBot's philosophy - AI that auto-schedules your tasks into your calendar - and for working professionals it is genuinely good. But it is built for meetings and work calendars, has no student exam workflow, and costs **$19/month billed yearly or $29 month-to-month (roughly Rs 1,600-2,500 a month)**. For an Indian student, that is a year of PlanBot's top tier every month. It earned its reputation in Silicon Valley; your JEE prep is not its use case.

## 6. Google Calendar - The Free Baseline

Time-block your class schedule, set revision reminders, share timetables with friends - and it costs nothing forever. Every app above assumes Calendar exists. It is not AI planning (it never tells you what to do), but as the free foundation under your study system, nothing beats it.

## The Decision, Compressed

- **You want the app to build and adjust your study day around exams** - PlanBot (free to start, INR pricing)
- **You already plan well and want a beautiful task list** - Todoist or TickTick
- **You want a study knowledge base** - Notion
- **You are a working professional wanting AI scheduling** - Motion, or PlanBot's professional persona at a fraction of the price
- **You want free, forever** - Google Calendar + PlanBot's free web tools

Prices move; judgment does not: pick the app that removes planning work from your day, not the one that gives you another system to maintain.
