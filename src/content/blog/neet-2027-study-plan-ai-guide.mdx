## Why NEET Preparation Needs a Smarter Approach

NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) is one of the most competitive exams in India, with over 20 lakh aspirants competing for approximately 1 lakh MBBS seats. The syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry, and Biology from Classes 11 and 12 — that's over 90 chapters across three subjects.

**The challenge isn't intelligence. It's planning.**

Most NEET droppers don't fail because they can't understand the concepts. They fail because they didn't allocate their time properly — spending too much on strong subjects, ignoring weak chapters, or revising too late.

## The 12-Month NEET 2027 Master Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-4: May - August 2026)

**Objective:** Complete NCERT thoroughly + build concept clarity

**Daily Study Target:** 6-8 hours

**Subject Split:**
- Physics: 2 hours/day
- Chemistry: 2 hours/day (1hr Physical + 1hr Organic/Inorganic alternating)
- Biology: 2-3 hours/day

**Key Actions:**
1. Read NCERT line by line — this is non-negotiable for NEET
2. Make short notes for each chapter
3. Solve NCERT exercises and exemplar problems
4. Watch concept videos for difficult topics

**How PlanBot Helps:** Tell PlanBot *"I'm preparing for NEET 2027, starting fresh. I can study 7 hours daily. I'm weak in Physics and Organic Chemistry."* The AI creates a daily schedule that front-loads weak subjects during peak energy hours.

### Phase 2: Strengthening (Months 5-8: September - December 2026)

**Objective:** Complete reference books + topic tests

**Daily Study Target:** 8-10 hours

**Subject-Wise Strategy:**

**Physics:**
- DC Pandey or HC Verma for problem-solving
- Focus on: Mechanics, Optics, Modern Physics, Electrostatics
- Daily: 20 numerical problems minimum

**Chemistry:**
- Physical: N. Avasthi problems
- Organic: MS Chauhan for reaction mechanisms
- Inorganic: NCERT + VK Jaiswal for assertion-reason
- Memorize: Periodic table trends, name reactions, reagent charts

**Biology:**
- Biology is 50% of the NEET paper (360 marks)
- Focus on NCERT: Genetics, Ecology, Human Physiology, Plant Physiology
- Use diagrams — NEET loves diagram-based questions
- Minimum: 3 chapters revision per week

**How PlanBot Helps:** The AI schedules your study blocks with proper breaks (Pomodoro-style), inserts revision slots for previously completed chapters, and ensures all three subjects get proportional attention.

### Phase 3: Revision & Mock Tests (Months 9-12: January - April 2027)

**Objective:** Revise everything + take 50+ mock tests

**Daily Study Target:** 10-12 hours

**Weekly Structure:**
- Monday-Friday: Revision + chapter tests
- Saturday: Full mock test (180 questions, 200 minutes)
- Sunday: Mock test analysis + weak area revision

**Mock Test Strategy:**
1. Start with subject-wise mocks (Physics only, Chemistry only, Biology only)
2. Move to full-length mocks by month 10
3. Analyze every mock: note wrong answers, revision gaps, time management issues
4. Target: Minimum 50 full-length mocks before NEET 2027

## Subject-Wise Chapter Priority

### Biology (50% of NEET — Most Important)

**High-Yield Chapters (study first):**
- Human Physiology (Digestion, Circulation, Respiration, Excretion, Neural)
- Genetics & Evolution (Molecular basis, Mendelian, Evolution)
- Ecology & Environment (Organisms, Ecosystem, Biodiversity)
- Cell Biology (Cell cycle, Biomolecules)
- Plant Physiology (Photosynthesis, Respiration, Growth)

**Medium-Yield:**
- Reproduction (Human + Plant)
- Biotechnology (Principles + Applications)
- Animal Kingdom classification

### Physics (25% of NEET)

**High-Yield:**
- Mechanics (Newton's Laws, Work-Energy, Rotational Motion)
- Electrostatics & Current Electricity
- Optics (Ray + Wave)
- Modern Physics (Photoelectric, Atomic Models, Nuclear)
- Thermodynamics

**Medium-Yield:**
- Waves & Oscillations
- Electromagnetic Induction
- Semiconductor Devices

### Chemistry (25% of NEET)

**Physical Chemistry:**
- Thermodynamics & Thermochemistry
- Chemical Equilibrium
- Electrochemistry
- Chemical Kinetics
- Solutions

**Organic Chemistry:**
- GOC (General Organic Chemistry)
- Hydrocarbons
- Alcohols, Phenols, Ethers
- Aldehydes, Ketones, Carboxylic Acids
- Amines, Biomolecules, Polymers

**Inorganic Chemistry:**
- Chemical Bonding
- Coordination Compounds
- p-Block Elements
- d & f Block Elements
- Periodic Properties

## Common Mistakes NEET Aspirants Make

1. **Ignoring NCERT** — NEET is 95% NCERT-based. Don't prioritize coaching modules over NCERT.
2. **Starting mock tests too late** — Begin subject-wise mocks from month 6, not month 11.
3. **Unequal subject allocation** — Biology is 50% of the paper but many students give it only 30% of study time.
4. **Not tracking progress** — Without data on what you've covered, you'll keep revising the same topics.
5. **Studying without a schedule** — Random studying leads to random results. Use AI planning.
