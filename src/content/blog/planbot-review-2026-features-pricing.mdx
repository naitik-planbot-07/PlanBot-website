## What is PlanBot?

PlanBot is an **AI-powered daily planner app** that uses advanced language models to generate personalized daily schedules. Instead of manually creating to-do lists, you describe your goals to the AI, and it creates an optimized plan with time blocks, priorities, breaks, and activity sequencing.

The app combines AI planning with **gamification** (streaks, XP, levels) to keep you consistently engaged with your goals.

## Core Features Review

### 🤖 AI Plan Generation — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

This is PlanBot's killer feature. The natural language interface is intuitive — you simply type what you want to achieve, and the AI creates a structured daily plan.

**Example input:** *"I have a physics exam in 5 days, need to finish a coding project by Friday, and want to workout 3 times this week. I'm free from 9 AM to 10 PM."*

**AI output:** A complete daily plan with study blocks, coding sessions, workout slots, meals, and breaks — all time-blocked and prioritized.

**What we love:**
- Multiple AI tiers to choose from (Basic → Standard → Advanced → Premium progression)
- Plans are genuinely personalized, not generic templates
- Regeneration is seamless when plans change
- Natural language understanding is excellent

**What could improve:**
- Limited to 2 plans/day on the free tier
- No real-time calendar sync yet

### 🔥 Streak System — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The streak system is deceptively simple but incredibly effective. Every day you complete your plan, your streak grows. Miss a day, it resets.

**What makes it great:**
- Visible streak counter creates real psychological stakes
- Streak freeze prevents total loss from one missed day
- Loss aversion kicks in after 10+ days (you CANNOT break it)
- Weekly and monthly streak milestones feel genuinely rewarding

This is the #1 retention feature in PlanBot. Users who build a 7-day streak have extremely high retention rates.

### 📊 Weekly AI Reports — ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Available on Elite and God tiers, weekly reports analyze your performance:

- Tasks completed vs. planned
- Subject/goal distribution
- Consistency patterns
- AI-generated improvement suggestions

**What we love:**
- Data-driven insights, not vague motivational quotes
- Trend analysis shows improvement over time
- Specific, actionable suggestions

**What could improve:**
- Only available on higher tiers
- No export to PDF option yet

### 🎮 XP & Leveling System — ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Complete tasks to earn XP, accumulate XP to level up. Simple RPG-style progression.

- Different tasks award different XP amounts
- Level milestones feel satisfying
- Provides an additional motivation layer alongside streaks

### 👤 Goal Personas — ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Selecting a persona (Student, Professional, Athlete, Entrepreneur) adjusts how the AI generates plans:

- **Student:** Focuses on study blocks, revision, exam prep
- **Professional:** Includes deep work, meetings, upskilling
- **Athlete:** Integrates training, recovery, nutrition
- **Entrepreneur:** Balances strategy, execution, health, networking

Personas are a smart way to personalize the AI without complex configuration.

### ⚡ Boost Packs — ⭐⭐⭐

One-time purchasable add-ons:
- Extra AI plans
- Streak freeze charges
- Premium themes

These provide monetization flexibility without subscription pressure. Good for users who want occasional upgrades without committing to a tier.
