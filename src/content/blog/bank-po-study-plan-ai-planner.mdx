## Why Banking Exams Reward Speed Over Depth

SBI PO and IBPS PO are the two biggest Probationary Officer recruitment exams in India — together selecting around 8,000-10,000 officers annually from over 20 lakh applicants.

Banking exams are **fundamentally different** from academic exams. The syllabus isn't vast — Class 10 math, basic reasoning, school-level English. What's brutal is the **time pressure**: SBI PO Prelims gives you 60 minutes for 100 questions. That's **36 seconds per question**, including reading time.

This changes your entire strategy. You don't need to *know* advanced concepts. You need to **recognize patterns instantly and solve mechanically**. That comes from one thing: volume of timed practice.

An AI planner like PlanBot structures this volume practice so you're not wasting hours deciding what to study.

## Understanding the Bank PO Exam Structure

### Prelims (Qualifying)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 35 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 min |

**Total:** 100 questions · 100 marks · 60 minutes · Sectional timing · Negative marking 0.25

### Mains (Merit-Deciding)

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reasoning & Computer Aptitude | 45 | 60 | 60 min |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 35 | 60 | 45 min |
| General/Economy/Banking Awareness | 40 | 40 | 35 min |
| English Language | 35 | 40 | 40 min |

**Total:** 155 questions · 200 marks · 180 minutes · Followed by Descriptive Test (letter + essay, 30 min, 50 marks)

**Then:** Interview (for final merit)

**Critical:** Mains is much harder than Prelims. DI sets are complex, Reasoning puzzles are multi-layered, and GA is unpredictable. Plan for Mains from day one.

## The 6-Month Bank PO Master Plan

### Phase 1: Fundamentals + Prelims Focus (Months 1-3)

**Objective:** Build concept clarity and Prelims-level speed

**Daily Study Target:** 5-6 hours

**Section Split:**

- Quant: 1.5-2 hours/day
- Reasoning: 1.5 hours/day
- English: 1 hour/day
- General/Banking Awareness: 45-60 min/day (foundation only)

**Section-Wise Strategy:**

**Quantitative Aptitude:**

- Fundamentals: Simplification, Number Series, Quadratic Equations (these are 10-15 free marks)
- Arithmetic: Percentages, Ratio, Profit-Loss, Time-Speed, SI/CI, Averages (50% of quant)
- DI: Tables, Bar, Line, Pie (start here in month 2)
- Target: 200+ questions daily by month 2

**Reasoning:**

- Puzzles & Seating Arrangement: 60% of reasoning — practice 2-3 puzzles daily
- Syllogism, Inequality, Coding-Decoding: 15-min daily drill
- Blood Relations, Direction, Order-Ranking: easy marks, don't neglect
- Target: 100+ questions daily by month 2

**English:**

- Reading Comprehension: 2-3 passages daily (this is 30-40% of English)
- Grammar: Error detection, Sentence Improvement, Cloze Test
- Vocab: 30 words daily (especially banking/finance terms)
- Target: daily reading habit is non-negotiable

**General Awareness:**

- Start Banking Awareness (RBI, types of accounts, banking terms)
- Daily 30 min current affairs
- Don't go deep yet — build foundation

### Phase 2: Speed Building + Sectional Mastery (Months 4-5)

**Objective:** Hit Prelims speed targets + start Mains prep

**Daily Study Target:** 6 hours

**What Changes:**

- **Timed sectional practice** becomes the core activity
- Start Mains-level DI and Reasoning
- Full-length Prelims mocks (3-4 per week)
- Banking Awareness goes deeper

**Speed Targets (Prelims):**

- Quant: 30+ correct in 20 min (need to attempt smartly, skip hard)
- Reasoning: 28+ correct in 20 min
- English: 25+ correct in 20 min

**The Art of Skippage:** Banking exams reward knowing what to *skip*. A puzzle that takes 5 minutes for 5 marks is a trap. Practice identifying and skipping time-sinks. This is where most aspirants fail.

**How PlanBot Helps:** *"Prelims score stuck at 65/100. Time runs out in Quant."* PlanBot reallocates to Quant speed drills and DI shortcuts for two weeks.

### Phase 3: Mains Mastery + Mock Series (Month 6)

**Objective:** Mains-level preparation + full mock series + Descriptive prep

**Daily Study Target:** 6-7 hours

**What Changes:**

- Full-length Mains mocks (2-3 per week)
- Complex DI sets (caselets, missing data, arithmetic-based)
- Advanced puzzles (floor, scheduling, multi-variable)
- Descriptive Test prep: write 1 letter + 1 essay daily
- GA: last 4 months current affairs revision + Banking Awareness

**Descriptive Test Tips:**

- Format matters (formal letter structure)
- Practice on a keyboard — typing speed is critical (30 min for 2 pieces)
- Read model essays to build structure

## Common Bank PO Mistakes That Cost Selection

- **Treating Prelims and Mains as the same exam** — Mains difficulty is 2x; prepare accordingly
- **Not practicing skippage** — trying to attempt every question kills your score
- **Ignoring the Descriptive Test** — qualifying, but many strong candidates fail it
- **GA cramming** — banking awareness takes 3-4 months of daily effort
- **No timed practice** — solving 50 questions in an hour ≠ solving 50 in 20 minutes

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I prepare for SBI PO and IBPS PO simultaneously?** Yes — syllabus is 95% identical. Prepare once, write both. IBPS typically follows SBI by 2-3 months.

**Is Bank PO harder than SSC CGL?** Different. SSC is syllabus-deep; Bank PO is speed-focused. Bank PO Quant is easier, but time pressure is intense.

**How fast should my typing be for the Descriptive Test?** Aim for 30-35 WPM minimum. You need to write ~400 words (essay) and ~150 words (letter) in 30 minutes.
