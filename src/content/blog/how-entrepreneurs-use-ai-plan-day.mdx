## The Unique Productivity Challenge for Entrepreneurs

Entrepreneurs don't have a boss telling them what to do. That sounds like freedom — until you realize it means **every decision is yours**. What to build, who to hire, which emails to answer, when to exercise, what to eat, when to sleep.

The average founder makes **35,000 decisions per day** (according to research from Cornell University). By 3 PM, their decision-making quality is degraded. By 7 PM, they're making bad choices about food, exercise, and sleep — the very things that fuel tomorrow's performance.

**AI planning eliminates the lowest-value decisions so you can focus on the highest-value ones.**

## The Founder's AI Planning Framework

### 2. Deep Work Blocks: Protect Your Creative Hours

Cal Newport's research on *Deep Work* shows that knowledge workers produce their best output in uninterrupted 90-minute blocks. But most founders never protect these blocks because "something always comes up."

PlanBot solves this by:
- Scheduling deep work in your highest-energy window (usually 9-11 AM)
- Batching meetings and calls into a separate block (usually 1-4 PM)
- Leaving the last 2 hours for email, admin, and planning tomorrow

## Common Scheduling Mistakes Founders Make

### Mistake 1: Back-to-Back Meetings
Every meeting requires 15-20 minutes of context switching. Three back-to-back meetings don't take 3 hours — they take 4+ hours when you include recovery time. PlanBot automatically adds buffer time between meetings.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Health
"I'll exercise when the startup is successful" is a recipe for burnout. PlanBot schedules health activities as non-negotiable blocks — just like investor meetings.

### Mistake 3: Reactive Scheduling
Opening email first thing and letting it dictate your day means you're working on other people's priorities. PlanBot ensures your highest-impact tasks are scheduled BEFORE reactive work.

### Mistake 4: Weekend Work Without Recovery
Some weekend work is inevitable for founders. But PlanBot ensures that if you work Saturday, Sunday includes recovery blocks and personal activities.
