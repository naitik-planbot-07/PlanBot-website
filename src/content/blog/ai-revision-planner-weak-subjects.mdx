### 3. The End of "Planning Fatigue"

Students waste hours every Sunday trying to create the "perfect" weekly schedule. An AI app creates your schedule in seconds every morning, adapting it to how much sleep you got and how much energy you have.

## How to Create a Personalized NEET/JEE Study Schedule for Weak Subjects

Here is the exact workflow to use AI to target your weak chapters:

**Step 1: Set Your Persona** When setting up PlanBot, select the **Student** persona. This tells the underlying algorithm to prioritize study blocks, exam preparation, and academic focus.

**Step 2: Input Your Syllabus & Weaknesses** Use the "Active Goals" feature (available in Pro/Elite tiers) to log your core subjects. If Physics is your weak subject, set it as a high-priority goal.

**Step 3: Let AI Generate Your Day** Every morning, the AI will generate your daily plan. Because you marked Physics as a high priority, it will automatically place your Physics revision block in your highest energy window (usually early morning).

**Step 4: The Feedback Loop** At the end of the day, mark how much of the plan you completed. If you didn't finish the Biology revision, the AI will automatically carry it over and adjust tomorrow's schedule so you don't fall behind.
