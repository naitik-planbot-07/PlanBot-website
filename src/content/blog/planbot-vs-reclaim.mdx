**Quick Answer:** PlanBot and Reclaim.ai are both AI planners with fundamentally different approaches. Reclaim.ai is a calendar-centric tool that protects focus time, schedules habits, and auto-syncs with Google Calendar/Outlook — best for knowledge workers who already use a calendar heavily (starts at $18/month). PlanBot is a goal-centric mobile planner that generates complete daily plans from natural language, with gamification and weekly AI reports — best for students, professionals, and athletes (Free to ₹249/month). Choose Reclaim to protect focus time in an existing calendar; choose PlanBot to generate a plan when you don't have one.

</div>

Reclaim.ai made its name on a single, brilliant idea: **protect your focus time from meeting creep.** You tell Reclaim you need 4 hours of deep work, and it defends those blocks — moving them around your meetings automatically, but never letting them disappear. For remote workers drowning in Zoom calls, that's a lifesaver.

But Reclaim solves a *specific* problem: calendar defense. It assumes you already have a calendar, tasks, and habits — you just need an AI to fit them together without losing your focus time.

PlanBot solves a *different* problem: plan generation. It assumes you have a goal but no plan — and builds the entire daily schedule from scratch. These aren't competing tools so much as tools for different stages of productivity.

Let's break it down.

## The Core Approach Difference

**Reclaim's approach:** Start with your calendar (Google Calendar or Outlook). Add tasks and habits. Reclaim's AI finds gaps and fills them intelligently, prioritizing your focus time. When meetings get added, Reclaim reshuffles your tasks to protect what matters.

**PlanBot's approach:** Start with your goal. Tell PlanBot what you're working toward. Its AI generates a complete time-blocked day — what to do, when, and for how long — with built-in breaks, gamification, and adaptation.

| Dimension | Reclaim.ai | PlanBot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starting point** | Your existing calendar | Your goal (natural language) |
| **What the AI does** | Fits tasks into calendar gaps | Generates the whole plan |
| **Best for** | Calendar-heavy knowledge workers | Goal-driven individuals without a plan |
| **Calendar integration** | Deep (Google Calendar, Outlook) | Plan-centric (not calendar-tied) |
| **Gamification** | Habit tracking (basic) | Streaks, XP, levels, freeze |
| **Platform** | Web + mobile (calendar-first) | Mobile-first (Android) |
| **Pricing** | Free (basic) → $18/month | Free → ₹249/month (~$3) |

## Feature-by-Feature Comparison

### 1. Focus Time Protection

**Reclaim:** This is Reclaim's flagship feature, and it's genuinely excellent. You define a "Focus Task" (e.g., "Write report, 4 hours"), and Reclaim holds that time across your calendar. If a meeting gets booked over it, Reclaim moves the focus block — not the meeting. You can set it to "defend" (don't move) or "flexible" (move as needed).

**PlanBot:** PlanBot generates focus blocks as part of your daily plan, but it doesn't sync with an external calendar to defend them against meeting invasions. It's a planner, not a calendar defender.

**Winner:** Reclaim, decisively, for focus-time defense.

### 2. Habit Scheduling

**Reclaim:** Habits are first-class. You set a habit ("Read 30 min daily, 3x/week"), and Reclaim schedules it into your calendar automatically, adjusting around meetings. Great for consistency.

**PlanBot:** Habits are built into your daily plan and reinforced by the gamification system (streaks, XP). PlanBot doesn't schedule habits around an external calendar, but it makes habit *consistency* more rewarding through gamification.

**Winner:** Reclaim for calendar integration; PlanBot for habit motivation.

### 3. Plan Generation

**Reclaim:** Reclaim does *not* generate plans. You must already know your tasks and habits. Reclaim's job is to fit them into your calendar smartly.

**PlanBot:** This is PlanBot's superpower. You say *"I'm preparing for UPSC, 8 hours daily, weak in Polity,"* and PlanBot generates a complete day — Polity block in the morning (peak focus), History after lunch, Current Affairs evening, breaks scheduled. The thinking is done for you.

**Winner:** PlanBot, decisively, for plan generation.

### 4. Calendar Integration

**Reclaim:** Deep, two-way sync with Google Calendar and Outlook. This is its foundation — if you don't use one of these calendars, Reclaim has little to offer.

**PlanBot:** Plan-centric. It generates your plan as time blocks within the app. It doesn't require (or deeply integrate with) an external calendar. This is liberating if you don't live in Google Calendar — but limiting if you do.

**Winner:** Reclaim for calendar users; PlanBot for plan-first users.

### 5. Gamification & Motivation

**Reclaim:** Minimal. Habit streaks are tracked, but there's no XP, levels, or achievement system.

**PlanBot:** Full gamification — streaks, XP, levels, achievements, and streak freeze. Built for users who need *consistency motivation*, not just scheduling.

**Winner:** PlanBot, for users who need motivation to stick to plans.

### 6. Weekly AI Reports

**Reclaim:** Analytics on how you spent your time vs. planned. Useful, but descriptive.

**PlanBot:** Elite+ users get a personalized AI report every Monday — accomplishments, gaps, time distribution, and *specific improvement suggestions*. More prescriptive.

**Winner:** PlanBot, for actionable analysis.

### 7. Pricing

**Reclaim:** Free (basic, limited) → Starter $10/month → Business $18/user/month.

**PlanBot:** Free (2 plans/day) → Pro ₹99/month → Elite ₹129/month → God ₹249/month.

**Winner:** PlanBot, by a wide margin — especially for individuals outside the US/EU.

## Who Should Choose Reclaim.ai?

Reclaim is genuinely excellent for a specific user:

- **Knowledge workers** who live in Google Calendar or Outlook
- **Remote workers** fighting meeting overload
- **People who already have tasks and habits** and just need them scheduled smartly
- **Teams** that need shared scheduling
- **Users whose main problem is "I can't find focus time"**

If your day is "10 meetings, 15 tasks, and no time to think," Reclaim reclaims that time beautifully.

## Who Should Choose PlanBot?

PlanBot is built for a different user:

- **Students** preparing for exams who need a study schedule generated
- **Professionals** who want a daily plan covering work, fitness, and goals
- **Athletes and habit-builders** who need gamified consistency
- **Mobile-first users**, especially in India
- **Anyone whose problem is "I don't know what to do today"** (not "I can't fit it in")

If your day is "I have a goal but no plan," PlanBot generates that plan instantly.

## The Honest Take

Reclaim and PlanBot rarely compete for the same user. The decision comes down to one question:

**Do you already have a plan and need to protect it — or do you need a plan generated?**

- If you have tasks and habits but can't fit them in your calendar → **Reclaim**.
- If you have a goal but don't know how to structure your day → **PlanBot**.

For students especially, PlanBot's plan generation is the rarer, more valuable feature. You can protect focus time with discipline; you can't generate a JEE study schedule with discipline alone.

## Can You Use Both?

Yes. Some professionals use Reclaim for work-meeting defense and PlanBot for personal goals and habits. They don't conflict. But for most individual users, one tool suffices — pick the one that matches your actual problem.

## The Verdict

Reclaim.ai is the best AI planner for **calendar defense and focus-time protection** in Google Calendar/Outlook workflows. If meeting overload is your enemy, Reclaim is the answer.

PlanBot is the best AI planner for **goal-driven plan generation and habit consistency** on mobile. If "what should I do today?" is your question, PlanBot answers it.
